# Data retention and security

## Retention
Ticket data is kept for 24 months after a ticket is closed, then deleted. Audit logs are kept for 12 months on the Growth plan and 36 months on the Enterprise plan.

## Encryption
Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest with AES 256. Customer managed encryption keys are available on the Enterprise plan.

## Data export and deletion
Admins can export all data as JSON from Settings and Data. A deletion request removes workspace data within 30 days.

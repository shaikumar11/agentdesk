# Support

## Support hours and channels
Starter and Growth customers get email support on business days with a first response target of 24 hours. Enterprise customers get 24 by 7 support with a first response target of 1 hour for priority 1 issues.

## Escalation
Priority 1 means production is down. Priority 2 means a major feature is degraded. Priority 3 is for questions and minor issues. Priority 1 and 2 issues should be raised as a support ticket with the request id.

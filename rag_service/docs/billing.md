# Billing

## Plans and pricing
The Starter plan costs 12 dollars per seat per month. The Growth plan costs 29 dollars per seat per month. The Enterprise plan uses custom pricing. Annual billing gives a 20 percent discount.

## Invoices
Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and emailed to the billing contact. Payment is due within 15 days. Refunds are available within 14 days of the first payment.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and the charge is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
